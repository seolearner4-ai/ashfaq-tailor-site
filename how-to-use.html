Guide

How to Use This Website

A quick guide to finding what you need, choosing your style and getting in touch with Ashfaq Tailor.

Five easy steps

From browsing to your first fitting

  1. Browse our services

    Open Services to see what we stitch: shalwar kameez, kurta, shirts, pants and trousers, waistcoats and alterations.

  2. See our work

    Open Our Work to look at real stitching. Use the category buttons to filter, and tap any photo to see it larger.

  3. Choose your style

    On the Home page, use Choose Your Style. Pick a garment and a fitting, and your WhatsApp message is written for you.

  4. Message or call us

    Tap the green WhatsApp button, or Call Now. You can also use the inquiry form on the Contact page; it opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send.

  5. Visit the shop

    Open the Shop page, tap the map or Get Directions, and come to Iqbal Town, GM Abad. Measurements are normally taken at the shop so the fit is right.

Buttons explained

What each button does

WhatsApp

Opens WhatsApp with a message ready to send to the shop.

Call Now

Calls the shop phone number. It works on phones.

Get Directions

Opens Google Maps with directions to the shop.

The map

Tap the map and the shop location opens in Google Maps in a new tab.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need an account to use this website?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Just browse and message us.

Is my information saved on the website?

No. The inquiry form only builds a WhatsApp message in your browser. Nothing is saved on this website.

Can I pay online here?

No. This website does not take online payments. Orders, prices and delivery time are talked through with the shop on WhatsApp, by phone, or in person.

What should I send in my first message?

Tell us which garment you want, the style you like (a photo helps), and when you would like to visit the shop.

Ready to start? Message us on WhatsApp

Message us on WhatsApp, call, or visit the shop in Iqbal Town. We will help you with style, fitting and measurements.

Privacy Policy

How the Ashfaq Tailor website handles your information.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Ashfaq Tailor website. We keep things simple: this is a small tailoring shop website, and we collect very little.

1. Information you share with us

This website has no accounts or sign-up. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, or use the inquiry form, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, phone number and the clothes you want stitched. We use them only to reply to you and to serve your tailoring order.

2. The inquiry form

The inquiry form does not send your details to our servers. It prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser and opens WhatsApp. Nothing you type is stored on this website. What you send in WhatsApp is handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we turn on a website analytics tool in the future, it would show us general usage, such as which pages are visited, and we would update this page.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Maps: the map on some pages loads from Google, and Google may collect technical information such as your IP address when it loads. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp: buttons open WhatsApp, which is run by a separate company with its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting: the company that hosts this website may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

5. Photos on this website

We show photos of our shop and our stitching work. If you believe a photo shows your clothes or your image and you would like it removed, contact us and we will look into it.

6. How we protect and keep information

We keep customer details only as long as needed to serve your order and answer your questions, and we do not sell your information.

7. Children

This website is meant for adults arranging tailoring. We do not knowingly collect information from children through the website.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to stop messaging you, or to delete the details you sent us, by contacting us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

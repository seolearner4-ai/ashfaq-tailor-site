Choosing Fabric for Shalwar Kameez in Faisalabad’s Weather

How to pick the right fabric for summer and winter in Faisalabad, from light cotton and lawn to warmer cloth, plus a few tips to keep your stitched clothes looking good.

By Ashfaq Tailor · 24 October 2026 · All articles

Faisalabad has long hot summers and cooler winters, so the fabric you choose changes how comfortable your clothes feel. Here are some simple things to think about before you buy cloth.

For hot weather

Light, breathable fabrics such as cotton and lawn are usually the most comfortable when it is hot. They let air pass through and feel lighter on the skin. Very thick or tightly woven cloth can feel heavy in the heat.

For cooler weather

In winter, many people prefer thicker cloth that holds some warmth. A heavier fabric also tends to hang differently, so tell your tailor what you chose so the cutting and stitching suit it.

Think about care

  • Ask whether the fabric may shrink when washed, and plan for it.
  • Some fabrics crease easily. If you do not like ironing often, pick one that stays smooth.
  • Dark and bright colours can fade, so wash them gently.

Bring the fabric to your tailor early

Showing the cloth before stitching lets the tailor tell you how much is needed, and which collar, placket and cuff styles will look best on it.

Tip: if the fabric might shrink, ask your tailor whether it should be washed once before stitching.
